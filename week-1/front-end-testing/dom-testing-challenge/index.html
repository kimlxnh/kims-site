A little bit about me...

I'm a British-born Vietnamese, who was born and raised in London. I love to plan holidays, and design these itineraries using Canva. I'd love to do more long-haul destination holidays, as I've had more short-haul weekend trips this year like to Lisbon, Lille and along the Amalfi Coast. I love to try out new restaurants with my friends, and cook on my free days. I used to bake a lot during Covid, but I feel like I enjoy more savoury dishes now.
